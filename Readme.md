# IBM HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The IBM HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze employee data and provide insights into workforce demographics, attrition, employee satisfaction, compensation, business travel, and work-life balance.

The dashboard helps HR teams and management identify patterns affecting employee retention, performance, and overall organizational health through data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor employee attrition and retention trends
- Analyze workforce demographics
- Evaluate employee satisfaction and engagement
- Understand compensation and salary distribution
- Examine the impact of travel, overtime, and work-life balance
- Support HR decision-making with actionable insights

---

## 📌 Key KPIs

- Employee Count
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Years at Company
- Average Working Years
- Overtime Percentage
- Average Salary Hike %
- Gender Distribution

---

## 📂 Dashboard Pages

### Dashboard 1: Employee Demographics Analysis

Provides insights into employee distribution across:

- Age Groups
- Gender
- Departments
- Job Roles
- Monthly Income by Gender

**Key Findings:**
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Male Employees: 60%
- Female Employees: 40%
- Largest age group is around 30 years.

---

### Dashboard 2: Attrition Analysis

Focuses on employee turnover patterns.

**Visualizations:**
- Attrition Count
- Department-wise Employee Count
- Overtime vs Attrition
- Years at Company Analysis
- Attrition by Years at Company

**Insights:**
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Employees working overtime show higher attrition.
- Most employees leave during their early years in the organization.

---

### Dashboard 3: Compensation & Salary Analysis

Analyzes employee income and salary growth.

**Visualizations:**
- Average Income by Department
- Average Salary by Job Level
- Salary Hike vs Performance
- Income by Job Role
- Stock Option Distribution

**Insights:**
- Higher job levels receive significantly higher salaries.
- Sales Executives contribute the highest total income.
- Salary hikes positively correlate with performance ratings.

---

### Dashboard 4: Employee Satisfaction & Work-Life Balance

Examines factors affecting employee engagement.

**Visualizations:**
- Job Satisfaction
- Environment Satisfaction
- Job Involvement
- Work-Life Balance
- Attrition vs Work-Life Balance

**Insights:**
- Majority of employees report moderate to high satisfaction.
- Better work-life balance is associated with lower attrition.
- Job involvement level 3 has the highest employee count.

---

### Dashboard 5: Travel & Lifestyle Analysis

Studies the impact of travel and personal factors.

**Visualizations:**
- Business Travel Distribution
- Distance from Home
- Marital Status Impact
- Attrition vs Travel Frequency

**Insights:**
- Most employees travel rarely.
- Married employees represent the largest workforce segment.
- Frequent business travel can influence attrition trends.
